Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you use Typist. We aim to collect as little information as practical.

1. Typing practice and browser storage

Typing passages and calculations are processed in your browser. Typist does not ask you to provide a name or account to use the typing test. Your browser or hosting platform may process routine technical information needed to deliver and protect the site.

2. Server logs and security

The hosting provider may create technical logs such as IP address, browser details, request time, and requested pages for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. These records are governed by the hosting provider’s policies and retention practices.

3. Cookies and advertising

Typist may display advertising if an advertising service is enabled. Advertising partners, including Google when its services are used, may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads subject to their settings and policies. Learn more at Google’s advertising policies. Do not assume personalized ads or analytics are active unless those services have been enabled.

4. Contact form

If you send us a message, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, topic, and message) is processed by FormSubmit to deliver it to our contact inbox. Please do not send passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information. FormSubmit may process submissions under its own privacy terms.

5. Third-party links and your choices

External sites and services have their own privacy practices. You can manage cookies or clear local browser data through your browser settings. You may contact us to ask about a message you have submitted.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, email whoismrrobot877@gmail.com.
