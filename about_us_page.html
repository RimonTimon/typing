About the project

About Typist

Typist is a free, browser-based typing practice tool designed to help you build speed and accuracy in a calm, distraction-light workspace.

What you can do

Practice at your pace

Choose Normal or Advance passages and select a 60, 120, or 180 second session.

Track your progress

See live words per minute (WPM), accuracy, mistakes, and time remaining while you type.

Sound feedback

Optional keyboard-style audio feedback uses the browser’s Web Audio API.

Our goal

Typist aims to make regular practice simple and approachable for students, developers, writers, and anyone who wants to improve their typing. Results are estimates for practice, not a formal certification.

Questions or suggestions?

We welcome feedback and reports about accessibility, errors, or ideas that could make Typist better.
