About Typist

Typist was created with a single objective: to deliver a clean, distraction-free environment for improving typing speed, accuracy, and finger dexterity.

Relaxing Passages

We carefully select passages that focus on rhythm, mindfulness, and smooth transitions to keep stress levels low while typing.

Satisfying Feedback

Built-in Web Audio API synthesizes mechanical keyboard switch audio to give you instant tactile feedback for every keystroke.

Whether you are a developer, writer, student, or touch typing enthusiast, consistent practice on Typist will help you unlock faster words-per-minute (WPM) scores with high precision.
